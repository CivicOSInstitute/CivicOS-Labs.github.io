Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need programming experience?

No. The first book walks you through installing and running AI with zero coding. Later books introduce Python gradually — everything is explained step by step.

What hardware do I need?

A reasonably modern laptop or desktop. Mac (Apple Silicon or Intel), Windows, or Linux. At least 8 GB of RAM recommended; 16 GB+ for larger models. No GPU required — CPU-based models work fine for learning.

Is this for students, teachers, or general readers?

All three. Students get homework tools. Teachers get classroom assistants. General readers get a complete introduction to building with AI — no background required.
