CivicOS Labs / Formation / 2026

Why CivicOS Labs exists.

CivicOS Labs, LLC is the formation vehicle for CivicOS Institute — a planned nonprofit building open civic infrastructure for the public good.

The structure

A vehicle for formation, not the destination.

CivicOS Institute is the mission — a privacy-first, vendor-independent nonprofit designed to build open civic infrastructure for the public good.

But a nonprofit doesn't appear overnight. It requires incorporation, IRS determination, an independent board, bylaws, and governance infrastructure. That process takes time.

CivicOS Labs, LLC exists to do the work that can't wait: filing public records requests, publishing findings, building tools, accepting formation support, and proving the model before the nonprofit doors open.

The LLC is not the organization. It is the scaffold.

What happens next

Transfer when ready.

When CivicOS Institute is fully formed and its independent board of directors approves, CivicOS Labs, LLC will transfer to become a wholly owned subsidiary of the nonprofit entity.

This means:

  • All assets, intellectual property, tools, and records built by Labs transfer to Institute ownership
  • All restricted formation funds and pledges transfer to nonprofit governance
  • The LLC continues operating as a subsidiary under nonprofit board oversight
  • Financial controls, audits, and transparency requirements shift to nonprofit standards

The transfer is not optional — it is the designed purpose of the LLC.

What we're building now

Evidence first. Revenue parallel.

Evidence lane

Public records, transparency, accountability

FOIA filings across six Florida jurisdictions. Procurement data analysis. Public findings published when the evidence supports them. No claims before proof.

Revenue lane

Products, publications, founding support

Ebooks, tools, and a Founding 25 formation-support program to fund the build. All revenue flows through Labs until Institute can receive funds directly.

What CivicOS Labs is not

Clear boundaries.

  • Not a nonprofit. Labs is a for-profit LLC. Contributions are not tax-deductible.
  • Not a charity. Formation support is restricted funding for building the nonprofit, not a charitable donation.
  • Not permanent. The LLC exists to facilitate formation. Once Institute is ready, Labs becomes a subsidiary under nonprofit governance.
  • Not a shortcut. Everything we build — tools, evidence, revenue, reputation — has to survive the transfer and stand on its own under nonprofit oversight.
